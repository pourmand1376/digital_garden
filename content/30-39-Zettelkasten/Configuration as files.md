This is what i've understand as really important. 

Configurations for any app should be saved inside a readable text file. 

Why? I've been recently using iterm2 and it gave me a pop up and I accidentally changed a config of something I don't even remember. 

This has caused my Claude code app to act funny and texts overlay happened in my terminal. 

I searched a lot in my iterm2 configs and no matter how many changes I made, I couldn't revert that problem. 

This is why I migrated into Wezterm. A fully configurable terminal emulator via text!
